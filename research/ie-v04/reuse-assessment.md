# LRC Revised Acts: confirmed reuse terms

Updated on **2026-10-13**, following the project owner's receipt of a written LRC
reply dated **2026-10-08** to the enquiry about plain legislative text, including
linked historical versions, in public Git repositories and an API.

The LRC confirms that Revised Acts are available under CC BY 4.0 through Ireland's
open-data reuse framework. Its proposed website notice identifies the Oireachtas
(Open Data) PSI Licence, incorporating Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International. This closes the previously unresolved source-licence gate.
The owner retains the original correspondence; personal contact details and the
email are not reproduced in this public research repository.

The Revised Acts introduction page checked on 2026-10-13 did not yet display that
notice. The evidence for the LRC's adoption is its direct confirmation, rather
than a claim that the notice is already public or an inference from eISB's terms.

## Attribution and scope

- Credit the Houses of the Oireachtas, the electronic Irish Statute Book / Office
  of the Attorney General and the Law Reform Commission as the respective sources.
- Retain copyright and supplied warranty notices; link the Oireachtas Open Data
  PSI Licence and CC BY 4.0.
- Identify conversion to Markdown and structured history, preserve source URLs
  and revision cutoffs, and disclose source defects and image omissions.
- State that the output is neither the official text nor an official restatement,
  and that Legalize is independent and not endorsed by the source bodies.
- Retain the enquiry's scope: plain legislative text and historical versions;
  exclude LRC editorial annotations, commentary, logos and third-party material.

The generated country README carries this attribution and these notices. The
technical fidelity, history, health and consumer checks remain required before
publication; the source confirmation does not certify the generated corpus.
